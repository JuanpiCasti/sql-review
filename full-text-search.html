Full-text search, or, LIKE '%foo%' considered harmful

A search engine inside the database — stemming, ranking, phrase queries, and a GIN index that turns a sequential scan of millions of documents into a few milliseconds.

Postgres ships with a credible full-text engine: tokenisers for two dozen languages, configurable stemmers, phrase and proximity operators, and a ranking function. For the search bar on a moderately busy app, it is almost always enough — and it spares you the operational cost of running a second datastore. This chapter covers what's in the box.
